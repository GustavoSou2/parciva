Design principles

- Avoid generic SaaS aesthetics.
- Avoid excessive white space.
- Avoid excessive use of cards.
- Avoid identical card grids.
- Avoid unnecessary shadows.
- Avoid gradients unless they serve a clear purpose.
- Do not make every section a floating container.
- Maintain high information density.
- Use the entire available viewport.
- Use multiple surface levels to create depth.
- Prioritize hierarchy over decoration.
- Whitespace must be intentional.
- Create visual rhythm through spacing, typography, borders and background surfaces.
- Prefer composition over symmetrical grids.
- Use strong typography hierarchy.
- Keep the interface feeling like a real production application rather than a design concept.
- Every screen should have a clear visual focal point.
- Reuse the design system consistently across the application.


Before implementing a screen, determine the visual hierarchy and layout composition first. Do not default to a standard dashboard/card layout.

> Design this as if it were a product that already has thousands of active users.
